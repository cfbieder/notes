# Reviews

Dated review output that is still an **active input** — security, UX, CR, and documentation
reviews, including the output of the review agents in `.claude/agents/`.

- Naming: `<topic>_YYYY-MM-DD.md` (see [documentation-standard.md](docs/documentation-standard.md)).
- A review lives here while its findings are still open. Once every finding is actioned or
  consciously dropped, move it to [archive/](docs/archive/) — it becomes history, not input.
- Findings that turn into work get a CR or a roadmap entry; the review is the record of *why*,
  not the tracker.
